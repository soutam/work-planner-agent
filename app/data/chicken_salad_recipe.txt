# Classic Shredded Chicken Salad Recipe
# File: chicken_salad_recipe.txt
# Difficulty: Easy
# Prep Time: 15 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins | Total Time: 30 mins
# Servings: 4

================================================================================
SECTION 1: INGREDIENTS LIST
================================================================================
[METADATA]
Category: Ingredients
Type: Shopping List

[THE CHICKEN & POACHING LIQUID]
* 2 large chicken breasts (approx. 1 lb / 450g, boneless, skinless)
* 4 cups water or low-sodium chicken broth (enough to cover the chicken)
* 1 teaspoon salt
* 1/2 teaspoon black peppercorns
* 1 bay leaf

[THE SALAD BASE & CRUNCH]
* 2 stalks celery, finely diced (approx. 1/2 cup)
* 1/2 medium red onion, finely minced (approx. 1/4 cup)
* 1/2 cup green grapes, halved (optional, for a touch of sweetness)
* 1/4 cup sliced almonds or chopped pecans, toasted (optional, for crunch)

[THE CREAMY DRESSING]
* 1/2 cup high-quality mayonnaise
* 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
* 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
* 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt (adjust to taste)
* 1/4 teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper
* 2 tablespoons fresh dill or parsley, finely chopped

================================================================================
SECTION 2: STEP-BY-STEP COOKING INSTRUCTIONS
================================================================================
[METADATA]
Category: Instructions
Type: Process

STEP 1: COOK THE CHICKEN (POACHING METHOD)
1. Place the chicken breasts in a medium saucepan. 
2. Pour in the water or chicken broth until the breasts are completely submerged.
3. Add the 1 tsp salt, peppercorns, and bay leaf to the liquid.
4. Bring the liquid to a gentle boil over medium-high heat.
5. Once boiling, immediately reduce the heat to low, cover the pan with a lid, and let it simmer for 12 to 15 minutes. 
6. Check internal temperature: The chicken is done when it reaches 165°F (74°C) at its thickest part.
7. Remove chicken from the liquid and place it on a cutting board to cool for 10 minutes.

STEP 2: PREPARE THE CREAMY DRESSING
1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, lemon juice, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
2. Whisk the mixture thoroughly until completely smooth and uniform.
3. Stir in the chopped fresh dill or parsley. Taste and adjust seasoning if necessary.

STEP 3: SHRED AND ASSEMBLE
1. Using two forks (or your hands), shred the cooled chicken breasts into bite-sized pieces. Alternatively, cube the chicken with a chef's knife.
2. Add the shredded chicken, diced celery, minced red onion, halved grapes, and toasted nuts directly into the bowl with the dressing.
3. Use a large spoon or spatula to gently fold all ingredients together until the chicken and vegetables are evenly coated.

================================================================================
SECTION 3: SERVING OPTIONS & MACROS
================================================================================
[METADATA]
Category: Nutritional & Serving Info

[SERVING SUGGESTIONS]
* Classic Sandwich: Pile generously between slices of toasted sourdough or brioche with crisp lettuce.
* Low-Carb Option: Scoop into large butter lettuce cups or serve over a bed of mixed baby greens.
* Quick Snack: Serve alongside buttery crackers, pita chips, or sliced cucumbers.

[STORAGE INSTRUCTIONS]
* Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 3 to 4 days. 
* Do not freeze, as the mayonnaise-based dressing will separate and become watery.
